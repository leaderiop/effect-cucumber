<objective>
Wrap every step in its `BeforeStep`/`AfterStep` unit, with `AfterStep` guaranteed across the whole unit
— including a `BeforeStep` that fails before the step body ever runs.

Purpose: D-05 is the decision that makes `AfterStep` useful at all ("capture a screenshot / dump state
the instant a step fails"); without the guarantee it fires only on the happy path, which is the one
case nobody needs it for. D-06 and D-07 extend the combine-don't-mask and whole-unit-guarantee shapes
already locked for `After` in plan 07-04.
Output: the per-step unit in `buildScenarioEffect`, and the assertions that prove all three decisions.
</objective>

    > 0` branch reads like a micro-optimisation and creates a code path that only Features with hooks

    `runHookBatch([])` succeeds immediately and that is cheap enough.

    The `isUnresolved` branch keeps failing BEFORE the unit and is NOT wrapped: an unresolved step never
