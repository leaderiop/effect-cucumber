`# ADR-EC-025: The DataTable wrapper's accessors fail loudly, and both step arguments arrive in source order`.
Status `Accepted and implemented`, Date `2026-08-28`. Reference ADR-EC-008 as the decision this one
implements rather than supersedes — ADR-EC-008 said "decode through Schema" and its correction said
"this package must implement its own thin wrapper"; this ADR is the shape that wrapper took.

Record exactly three decisions, each with the alternative that was rejected and why:

**1. `raw()` is total; `hashes()` and `rowsHash()` return `Effect`.**
Rejected alternative: all three pure, with `@cucumber/cucumber`'s semantics — last cell wins on a
duplicate header, and `rowsHash()` reading the first two cells of a wider row. Rejected because a
silently-wrong result is the exact failure class `Validate.ts` and the ten-member
`LoadFeatureErrorReason` exist to eliminate, and because `@cucumber/gherkin` accepts a duplicate
header cell without complaint (fixture row F32). A step body is already a generator under
ADR-EC-005, so `yield* table.hashes()` costs one keyword.

**2. `DataTableError` is a third error class, not more members on the existing two.**
BEH-EC-014 closes `LoadFeatureErrorReason` at exactly ten with the words "drawn from exactly this
set", and `StepPatternError` is scoped to a pattern the step author wrote. A data-table failure is
raised at step-body time, against `.feature` content, long after loading succeeded, and carries a
`row`/`column` locator neither of the other two has. Its reason union is closed at four:
`DuplicateHeaderColumn`, `DuplicateRowKey`, `RowsHashRequiresTwoColumns`, `RowDecodeFailed`.

**3. A step carrying both arguments receives them in the source order `@cucumber/gherkin` recorded
on `argumentIndex`, as an ordered array.**
Rejected alternative: a fixed DocString-then-DataTable convention, or a
`{ docString, dataTable }` record. Rejected because `argumentIndex` is a real upstream fact
(verified: `1`/`2` for `docstring-and-datatable.feature`, `2`/`1` for the reversed
`datatable-before-docstring.feature`) and a record has no order at all, so every consumer would
have to re-derive it. Record the trap that makes this non-obvious: `argumentIndex` is set ONLY when
a step carries both arguments; a step with one has no such key, so any implementation reading it
needs a documented fallback.

Add a **Verified, not assumed** section — ADR-EC-022's own convention — listing the facts that were
reproduced against the installed dependencies rather than assumed, and naming the pin test that
