<objective>
Reconcile `spec/` with what now exists, and mark the phase's requirements.

Two of the corrections are not housekeeping. RESEARCH.md transcribed the spec's own published
signatures verbatim and compiled them:

- **BEH-EC-003 is vacuous.** Its generator branch is `Generator<any, A, any>` — a step yielding
  an unprovided `Db` against a `World`-only Layer compiles clean, exit 0. INV-EC-003 is
  decorative under the spec's own published signature. This is the exact booby trap the phase
  exists to avoid, sitting in the text an implementer is told to follow.
- **BEH-EC-002 erases `shared`.** Its union-argument form types `shared` as
  `Layer.Layer<any, any, never>` and binds `R` to `perScenario` alone, so ADR-EC-006's own
  motivating example — `shared: Database.layer` with steps that use `Database` — does not
  compile. A union in an inference position cannot be repaired by widening; the overload form
  is the fix.

AGENTS.md §1 makes correcting both mandatory in this phase, not optional cleanup: a code change
not reflected in `spec/` in the same change is incomplete.

Purpose: leave nothing in `spec/` that would produce a wrong implementation if copied, and
nothing claiming to be enforced that is not.
Output: three spec documents corrected, two status documents updated, four requirements marked.
