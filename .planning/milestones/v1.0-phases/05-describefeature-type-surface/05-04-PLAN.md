Deliver DSL-01's proof: the whole project's core value, asserted mechanically in a way that
cannot decay into a no-op while CI stays green.

Two committed fixtures — one satisfied, one starved of exactly one service — plus two new
assertions in `scripts/verify-tsgo-gate.sh`. The satisfied/starved PAIR, asserted in the same
script run, is the honest mechanism for roadmap success criterion 1's "removing a service from
an ambient Layer flips a previously-passing case to failing": no script that edits a file, no
mutable working tree, and the flip is re-asserted on every CI run instead of once.

fires `effect(missingEffectContext)` (`TS377004`), **not** `effect(missingLayerContext)`
(`TS377034`). Assertion 4's existing grep must not be copied verbatim into the new assertion —
doing so produces an assertion that fails for the wrong reason and invites someone to "fix" it
by weakening the grep.

Purpose: after this plan, a reordered step union or a degraded generic in `Dsl.ts` fails CI by
name instead of passing silently.
Output: two fixtures, two isolated configs, two new gate assertions.
