<objective>
Extend ADR-EC-016's build gate to cover DSL-05's compile-time boundary — roadmap success criterion 1
in the most literal sense: a step inside a `Rule` compiles against that Rule's own extra Layer, and the
IDENTICAL step body placed outside the Rule is a compile error, by name. Also cover D-01's second form
(a Scenario's own extra Layer) with an inline positive/negative pair in the SAME fixture, since it is
the identical mechanism at one less nesting level and 08-03 declares it as a separate interface
(`ScenarioRegistrar`) that could decay independently of `FeatureDsl.Rule`.

Purpose: `Dsl.ts`'s own house rule (note (a)) is that a decayed guarantee still compiles fine and
rejects nothing — nothing in this repo proves DSL-05's boundary holds unless a committed fixture pair
does. This plan needs only 08-03's type surface to exist; it does not depend on 08-05a's runtime wiring,
because `tsc` type-checks against `FeatureDsl<ROut>`'s declared shape regardless of what
`describeFeature`'s implementation currently does at runtime — the same reason 07-03 (the hook
tsgo-gate pair) could land right after Phase 7's own Dsl.ts plan.
Output: a satisfied/starved fixture pair, their two single-file tsconfigs, and gate assertions 12 and
13.
</objective>
